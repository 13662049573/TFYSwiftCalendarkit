# ``TFYSwiftCalendarkit``

A pure-Swift, production-focused calendar component for UIKit and SwiftUI.

## Overview

Use ``TFYSwiftCalendar`` for month or week presentation, horizontal or vertical paging, and compact continuous vertical scrolling. Configure content with ``TFYSwiftCalendarDataSource`` and interaction or per-day styling with ``TFYSwiftCalendarDelegate``.

The calendar normalizes selections to civil days in its configured `Calendar` and `TimeZone`. Its date math remains correct through daylight-saving transitions, and large date ranges use bounded page caching.
